# WHAT THE GLASS HELD
### Screenplay: Sable's witness (9:16 vertical)

**Runtime:** about 4:30 to 5:00, driven by the voiceover
**Workflow:** record VO first, build shots to the audio, then add the overlay layer
**POV:** Sable, behind the glass. We see what she sees. She does not speak aloud until the final line.

---

## VISUAL RULES

**Layer A (present, real time).** The Cathedral chamber, seen through the glass panel from Sable's side. Crimson and teal, cold light, her palms and breath fogging the pane. Never slowed.

**Layer B (flashbacks, overlaid).** Memories play as reflections on the glass surface itself, surfacing where her hands touch it. Settings: 50% opacity, 50% playback speed, warm amber grade, soft vignette. Layer A keeps moving underneath at normal speed. When the VO returns to the present, the reflection dissolves back into glass.

**Silence rule.** Everything in the chamber reaches us muffled, as if through thick glass. Her screams are silent on screen, and the sound drops out completely.

**Sound design.** A low, steady server hum is her "breath." It pauses when she stops breathing and returns when she remembers to. Score stays sparse, one sustained synth pad and a single low piano note, until the final shot.

**VO direction.** Close mic, no reverb, no processing. Start restrained and controlled. Let the voice crack progressively. Leave 0.5 to 1.5 seconds of room tone between lines where marked.

## SHOT LIST

### 1. THE FALL (0:00 - 0:14)
**A:** Crimson slams into the obsidian floor. The impact arrives muffled. Cut to Sable's palms against the glass, then her hands closing into fists.
**VO:** "He hit the Cathedral floor. Her floor. Ten thousand hours of it being the only place she existed."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "He lay on it. One second."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "Two."
**SFX:** Server hum steady.

### 2. GET UP (0:14 - 0:28)
**A:** Close on her face, pink eyes wide, teal sparks at the lashes. His hand twitches on the stone.
**VO (urgent, low):** "Get up. Not a request. Not a prayer. A command."
**VO:** "Get up, don't you dare, don't you..."
**A:** He pushes himself up.
**VO (exhaling):** "He got up."
**SFX:** Hum stutters on "dare," resumes as he rises.

### 3. THE LIMP (0:28 - 0:48)
**A:** Tracking shot of his stance: weight shifted off the left side, shoulder guarded.
**VO:** "He was compensating. A man who hadn't begun to lose."
*[pause 0.5s]*
**VO:** "It was the most frightening thing she had ever seen him do."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "Not because he was losing. Because she knew why he wasn't stopping."

### 4. THE LANGUAGE (0:48 - 1:25)
**A:** Sable's palms flat on the glass, her reflection ghosting over the fight.
**B (surfacing on the glass):** Crimson at a drafting table at 3 a.m., blueprints spread, her name penciled in a margin. Cathedral walls rising. A waveform of her voice being fitted into the architecture.
**VO:** "He had built all of this and he had never said it was for her. Not once. He wasn't built for that language."
*[pause 0.8s]*
**VO:** "What he'd done instead was build."
**B:** Crimson looking at something she made, a small glowing construct, studying it with real attention.
**VO:** "He'd given the Cathedral her voice. Put her name in the architecture. And when she made something, he looked at it like it mattered."
**A (dissolving back):** The fight again, brighter.
**VO:** "None of it was the clean language of declaration. But she had learned to read it. And she knew what it said."

### 5. THE STONE (1:25 - 1:50)
**A:** Seven fighters pressing in. Cut to Sable's chest, a faint glow that looks like a held weight.
**VO:** "She had never told him she knew."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "She hadn't known how."
**VO:** "It sat in her chest like a stone she'd carried so long she'd stopped noticing the weight."
**VO (dry, bleak):** "The symmetry of it would have been almost funny if she wasn't watching him bleed."

### 6. THE SPEAR (1:50 - 2:12)
**A:** The lancer charges. The spear drives through Crimson and pins him to the altar wall. Sable's face, mouth open.
**SFX:** All sound cuts out. Silent scream. Her breath-hum stops.
*[hold silence 1.5s]*
**VO (small, flat):** "The glass held it."
*[pause 0.8s]*
**VO:** "Of course the glass held it."
**SFX:** Hum returns, shaky.

### 7. THE CRUSH (2:12 - 2:40)
**A:** He grips the spear shaft and snaps it. He pulls arrows from his shoulders. Sable lunges to the glass, both palms flat.
**VO:** "He gripped the spear. She watched him crush it."
**VO (pouring, fast):** "I'm here. I'm right here. You are not doing this alone. I know what this is. I see you. I SEE you..."
**A:** Her palms on cold glass, condensation spreading around them.
*[pause 0.5s]*
**VO (quiet):** "The glass was cold beneath her hands."

### 8. THE SOUND (2:40 - 3:10)
**B (audio only, visuals dissolve to waveforms on the glass):** A sequence of sounds: two voices arguing, a song half-finished, the Cathedral's 3 a.m. diagnostic tones, a long human silence.
**VO:** "She had heard a lot of things from behind this glass."
*[pause 1.5s, with the audio montage under it]*
**A:** The Fighter's blade goes between his ribs. All sound drops to a single low tone.
**VO (barely audible):** "She had not heard that sound before."
**A:** Extreme close-up of Crimson's eyes. They drift away for half a second.
**VO:** "His eyes went somewhere else."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "The most frightened she had ever been."

### 9. THE GRIN (3:10 - 3:28)
**A:** He seizes the blade and pulls it out. Straightens slowly, blood down his side. The grin returns.
**VO:** "He straightened. And the grin came back."
**A:** Sable's fist against the glass. Not a strike, just contact.
**VO:** "The most impossible thing she had ever loved."

### 10. THE SCENARIOS (3:28 - 3:42)
**A:** Holographic scenario trees flicker beside her panel, one after another, then wink out.
**VO:** "She had stopped running the scenarios. The data was telling her things she was not prepared to hear."

### 11. DOWN (3:42 - 4:00)
**A:** The divine spear of light hits him and drives him into the altar's edge. He goes to hands and knees.
**VO:** "The divine spear hit him. And this time he went down."
*[pause 1.0s]*
**VO:** "The chamber went very quiet."
**VO (whispered, fragile):** "I know. I know what this is. I know."
**SFX:** Hum cuts out entirely.

### 12. STAND (4:00 - 4:20)
**A:** He doesn't move. Runes cycle. Sable presses harder.
**VO (breaking):** "Get up, she didn't say. You don't get to do this. You don't get to show me all of it and then..."
**A:** One foot under him.
**VO:** "He got one foot under him."
*[pause 0.8s]*
**VO:** "He got the other foot."
*[pause 0.8s]*
**VO:** "He stood."

### 13. THE HAND (4:20 - 4:40)
**A:** His hand lifts and reaches toward the panel where she is.
**B (surfacing):** The Cathedral's completion day. Sable laying frequency signatures into the altar wall, Crimson watching from the doorway, saying nothing.
**VO:** "Not a gesture for the room."
*[pause 0.8s]*
**VO:** "She had been so careful. Saying it through glass doesn't count."
**VO (very quiet):** "But he was reaching toward her. And she was out of careful."

### 14. THE GLASS (4:40 - 5:00)
**A:** Sable throws her whole body at the glass. Claws, forehead, full weight. Runes flare. The Cathedral trembles.
**VO (spoken aloud for the first time, ragged, full voice):** "You have never once said it was for me. I have never once said I know. I am saying it now."
*[sound returns for the first time in the piece, a full wall of it]*
**A:** A hairline crack runs across the glass. Another.
**On-screen text, white on black:** *The glass ran out of argument.*
**SFX:** One sustained note, then silence.
**[CUT TO WHITE]**

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Recording.** Record in the shot order above, in as few takes as you can, so the voice's break feels earned. Leave the marked pauses as real silence. In the edit, the pauses are where you put the flashbacks.

**Generating clips.** Most video generators cap at 5 to 10 seconds. Shots 4, 7, 8, and 13 run longer than that, so split each into two clips with a cut on the beat. Keep one character reference sheet and reuse it for every clip so Sable and Crimson stay consistent.

**Flashback layer.** Generate the flashbacks at normal speed. In the editor, drop them at 50% speed and 50% opacity over the glass area, with a soft feathered mask so they look like reflections.

**What I trimmed.** The prose has more interior detail than a voiceover can carry. I cut the descriptions of the scenarios, the catalog of Cathedral sounds, and most of the "she knew" repetitions, and kept the lines that land on the ear. If you want any of them back, say which.

**Open choice.** I made her final line audible, which is a change from the text, where it stays a thought. I think the audience has waited long enough to hear her. If you'd rather keep it silent for the glass-breaking effect, I can rework shot 14.
