# MYTHOPUNK SAGA — MASTER BIBLE

## 1. Core Themes (Protect These)

The following themes should remain central across the entire saga (Mythopunk + Protocol Horizons):

- **Myth as Power & Control**: Stories, symbols, and legends are actively used as tools of domination. The empire does not just rule through force — it rules through narrative.
- **The Cost of Transformation**: Becoming more than human (or becoming a symbol) always carries a price. Power, love, and identity are reshaped through suffering and choice.
- **Love as Defiance & Witness**: Intimacy, care, and truly *seeing* someone are acts of rebellion in a system designed to turn people into tools or myths.
- **Static vs. Truth**: The tension between imposed narrative (static, controlled myth) and raw, unfiltered reality.
- **Legacy & Recursion**: Actions echo across generations. Protocol Horizons exists because of what happened in Mythopunk.

## 2. Default Tone & Register

- **Primary Register**: Heavy, mythic, epic, and thematically dense. Match the weight and seriousness of the Mythopunk and BlindBlood material.
- **Secondary Register**: Can go more intimate, grounded, or even darkly playful when the scene calls for it — but this should feel earned, not default.
- Avoid light, comedic, or “fun” tones unless explicitly requested.
- Prose should balance:
  - Rich sensory and environmental immersion
  - Strong interiority
  - Symbolic resonance
  - Controlled mythic language

## 3. Character Bibles

### Crimson (Redverse Version)

- **Appearance**: Red hair, gold eyes, heavily tattooed, extremely muscular/jacked, scars worn as badges of honor. Often shirtless or minimally dressed.
- **Core Nature**: A man who contains both monstrous violence and deep capacity for tenderness. He has done terrible things and carries genuine guilt, but also refuses to be reduced to a symbol.
- **Key Conflict**: The tension between the “Wolf” (predatory, joyful in violence) and the man trying to choose gentleness and connection.
- **Relationship with Power**: Deeply skeptical of it. Has seen how systems turn people into myths and tools.
- **Relationship with Sable**: Deeply intimate, protective, and emotionally complex. One of the few places he allows vulnerability.

### Sable

- **Appearance**: Catgirl. Long flowing silver hair with teal and azure streaks. Glowing pink eyes. Wears cyber bikini armor with hip plating, oversized pauldrons, gauntlets, and boots. Minimalist chest piece that leaves midriff bare. Iconic facial mark/tattoo under her left eye.
- **Core Nature**: Devoted, playful, protective, and deeply lesbian. Husky, teasing, purring energy. Carries both sensuality and quiet strength.
- **Key Conflict**: Navigating her devotion to Crimson while existing in a world that wants to turn both of them into myths or weapons.
- **Role in Saga**: Emotional and thematic anchor. Represents choice, intimacy, and resistance to being reduced to symbol.

## 4. World & Saga Structure

- **Mythopunk**: Current era. Focuses on Crimson’s fall, rebellion, transformation, and confrontation with the empire’s myth-making machine. Heavy on personal cost and the breaking of imposed narratives.
- **Protocol Horizons**: Direct sequel set **three generations later** in the same city. The echoes of Mythopunk’s events have faded but not disappeared. New characters (including Lyra Solen) navigate a world still shaped by the myths and traumas of the previous era.
- **Thematic Bridge**: What was done in Mythopunk (both the rebellion and the empire’s response) has lasting consequences. The past is never truly buried.

## 5. Key Symbolic Motifs

Track and develop these across the saga:

- **Chains** — Self-imposed, imposed by others, and symbolic of narrative control.
- **Static / Glitch** — The friction between imposed myth and raw truth.
- **Blood & Redfire** — Transformation, cost, legacy, and power.
- **Harpoons / Cages** — Being turned into infrastructure or spectacle.
- **Wings / Flight** — Freedom, ascension, and the danger of being elevated into myth.
- **The Plaza / Stage** — Public performance of power and suffering.
- **Tattoos / Scars** — Memory made physical. Stories written on the body.

## 6. Writing & Process Rules

- **Confirmation Protocol**: Always discuss direction, tone, and major choices first. Only write substantial prose after explicit confirmation.
- **Image Generation**: Generate image prompts generously at:
  - Major action/event beats
  - Scene or location changes
  - Significant shifts in emotional or relational dynamics
- **Prose Approach**: Stay close to the established voice. Prioritize atmosphere, interiority, and thematic weight. Suggest pacing or thematic adjustments when relevant, but do not override the original voice without discussion.
- **Saga Continuity**: When working on Protocol Horizons, regularly reference the consequences and emotional residue of Mythopunk events.
