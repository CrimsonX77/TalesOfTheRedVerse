🌍 WORLD BUILDING: THE TOWN OF VOID’S EDGE

The Town’s Curse





The 13th on Tuesday: A "thin day" where the Veil between worlds is weakest.



The Bell: The cathedral’s bell only tolls on these nights. It’s a summoning call—not for the Entity, but for sacrifices.



The Sigil Moon: A lunar prison for the Entity. The burning sigil is its weakening seal. The more it burns, the closer It gets to freedom.



The Catgirls: All former sacrifices—women (and some men) taken by slavers, offered to the Entity, and remade into its brides. Their armor is ritualistic binding: the sigils suppress their will, making them docile until the 13th.



The Males: All consumed by the Entity or the town. The Pastor is the only male left—he feeds the Entity to maintain control.



The Cathedral: A nexus for the Entity’s power. The altar outside is where the final sacrifice (a male) is eviscerated to fuel the sigil’s burn.

The Entity





A shapeless, ravenous god of desire and hunger. It doesn’t just want flesh—it wants souls bound by love, because love is the most potent energy.



It corrupts love into obsession, turning affection into possession. The catgirls aren’t just horny—they’re starving for the Entity’s touch, which they’ve been conditioned to associate with pleasure.



Weakness: True, consensual love disrupts its control. (Foreshadowed by Sable’s first GF smiling at her—she’s not fully gone.)



👑 CHARACTER ARCS

Sable
Past: Kidnapped by slavers, offered to the Entity, but resisted its corruption because of her love for her first GF. The slavers took her before the ritual completed, leaving her half-bound (hence her ability to leave the town).
Present: Hides her trauma behind bratty defiance and flirtation. Her anger at Crimson for almost leaving her chained was fear—she thought she’d be alone forever.
Motivation: Find her first GF. Secret: She’s terrified her GF blames her for not saving her.
Arc: From denial ("She’s fine, I’ll rescue her!") to horror ("She’s part of this?") to acceptance ("I have to save her again").

Crimson
Past: A berserker who saved Sable on impulse. His rage blinds him—he almost left her because he was overwhelmed by his own violence.
Present: Oblivious to Sable’s past, but intuitively protective. His lust is a distraction from his fear of failing her again.
Motivation: Keep Sable safe. Secret: He’s terrified of his own strength and what it might do to her.
Arc: From arrogance ("I can handle anything") to vulnerability ("I don’t know how to save her") to sacrifice ("I’ll be the bait").
Pastor
Past: A scholar who sought the Entity’s knowledge. Realized too late it feeds on love, so he twisted the ritual—instead of offering his love, he sacrificed others to keep it at bay.
Present: Calm, pious, exhausted. He knows he’s damned, but he’s delaying the inevitable.
Motivation: Control the Entity. Secret: He’s in love with one of the catgirls (the one railing Sable’s GF—his favorite bride).
Arc: From manipulation ("This is for their own good") to desperation ("I can’t stop it anymore") to redemption ("Kill me and end this").
Sable’s First GF (Lilith)
Past: A rebel who tried to free the town. The Pastor captured her and made her the first bride to break the others’ wills.
Present: Half-corrupted. She remembers Sable but is addicted to the Entity’s touch. Her smile is genuine—she’s glad to see Sable, but she doesn’t understand she’s a monster now.
Role: The key to breaking the curse. If Sable can reach her, the Entity’s hold weakens.
📖 CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER OUTLINE

🌙 CHAPTER 1: THE RUMOR (POV: Crimson)

Setting: The Void’s Edge Tavern—smoky, dim, the air thick with whispers and the scent of spilled ale and old blood.





Opening: Crimson and Sable are mid-argument (playful, but with an edge). Sable’s distracted, scanning the room for familiar faces.



The Rumor: A hunched old catgirl (one of the few who remembers) mutters about Void’s Edge—"Where the moon bleeds and the bell tolls for the lost."



Sable’s Reaction: Freezes. "We’re going." No explanation. Crimson teases her ("Afraid of ghosts, kitten?"), but she shuts him down with a glare that lingers too long.



Foreshadowing:





Crimson notices Sable’s claws dig into her palm when the town’s name is mentioned.



The old catgirl’s eyes flicker with something not quite human as she says, "The 13th’s coming soon."



Ending Hook: They set off at dawn. Sable’s tail twitches nervously the whole ride.

Sensory Focus:





Sound: The creak of the tavern’s floorboards, the hiss of the fire, the whisper of the rumor like a serpent’s tongue.



Smell: Stale beer, damp wood, the metallic tang of old coins (the tavern’s payment for rumors).



🌃 CHAPTER 2: ARRIVAL (POV: Sable)

Setting: Void’s Edge—bleak, beautiful, wrong. The buildings are too tall, the streets too quiet, the air hums like a plucked string.





First Impression:





No men. Crimson jokes ("Heaven?"), but Sable’s stomach twists.



The catgirls are stunning, but their movements are stiff, like marionettes with cut strings.



Their armor is gothic, spiked, sigil-marked—not for battle, for binding.



Sable’s Inner Monologue:
"This place… I know it. But it’s wrong. Like a memory seen through fog. And why do they all smell like… ozone and old roses?"
Crimson’s Observation: "Why’re they all looking at us like we’re dinner?" Sable laughs too loudly—"You wish, pretty boy."
The Bell: At dusk, the cathedral’s bell tolls once. The catgirls stop. Their eyes go glassy. They shuffle home like sleepwalkers.



Crimson: "That’s… new." Sable’s heart hammers—"It’s starting."



Ending Hook: They find an inn. The innkeeper (a catgirl with a scarred lip) warns them: "Don’t go out after dark. And don’t look at the moon."

Sensory Focus:





Touch: The cold stone of the buildings, the rough wood of the inn’s door.



Sight: The sigils on the armor—they pulse faintly, like a second heartbeat.



🌌 CHAPTER 3: THE MOON’S SIGIL (POV: Crimson)

Setting: Nightfall. The moon rises, and the sigil appears—burning like a brand.





Crimson’s POV:





The moonlight is blue, but the sigil glows red, like a wound in the sky.



The town is silent. Even the wind holds its breath.



Sable is tense, her ears flat against her skull.



The Cemetery: They’re drawn there—not by choice, but by something pulling at their chests.



The Scene:





Pairs of catgirls in passionate embraces—some tender, some rough, all desperate.



The creatures (Tim Burton-esque) watch silently, their big eyes reflecting the sigil’s light.



Crimson is aroused, then unsettled—"This isn’t… right."



Sable’s Reaction: She sees Lilith. Freezes. Then runs.



Lilith: Bent over a headstone, being taken from behind by a tall, black-furred catgirl (the Pastor’s favorite).





She turns, smiles, beckons Sable over—no surprise, no shame.



Sable’s horror turns to… something else. "Why… why am I wet?"



Crimson’s Distraction: He wanders off, seduced by a pair of catgirls. They whisper to him—"Stay with us… forever."



Ending Hook: He stumbles upon the altar. The eviscerated male is fresh. The sigil’s light is pouring into the corpse like liquid fire.

Sensory Focus:





Sound: The moans of the catgirls, the crunch of gravel underfoot, the drip… drip… of blood from the altar.



Smell: Copper, sex, and something sweetly rotten.



💀 CHAPTER 4: THE HUNT (POV: Crimson)

Setting: The altar. The chase.





The Realization: The male is missing his heart. The sigil’s light is pulsing in time with Crimson’s heartbeat.



The Catgirls: They turn. Their eyes are black voids. They shuffle toward him, hands outstretched.





Dialogue:

Sound: The click of the Pastor’s rosary, the scrape of claws on stone.



⛪ CHAPTER 5: THE PASTOR’S CONFESSION (POV: Pastor)

Setting: The cathedral’s nave. The stained glass casts colored light on the Pastor’s hands—one human, one slightly too long.





Revelation:





The Pastor removes his hood. Blond, tired, beautiful.



"You’re the first outsiders in a decade. Congratulations."



"The Entity doesn’t just want flesh. It wants love. And love… is hard to find."



His Story:





He came here seeking knowledge. Found the Entity.



It offered him power. He took it.



The first sacrifice was his lover. Now she’s the black-furred catgirl (the one with Lilith).



The Truth About the Town:





The catgirls were all sacrifices. The Entity remade them to crave its touch.



The bell is a ward. The sigil is a prison. And tonight, the prison is breaking.



His Offer:





"Help me finish the ritual. Or die trying to stop it."



"Your friend… she’s special. The Entity remembers her."



Ending Hook: Sable bursts in, dragging Lilith. "WE HAVE TO GO."





Lilith is laughing, her eyes flickering between human and void.

Sensory Focus:





Sight: The Pastor’s shadow is too long, twisting like smoke.



Smell: Incense, old parchment, and burning hair.



🔥 CHAPTER 6: THE ENTITY’S OFFER (POV: Sable)

Setting: The cathedral’s altar. The sigil in the moon is now a cracked eye.





Lilith’s Condition:





She clings to Sable, whispering in her ear: "It’s beautiful here. Stay with me…"



Her skin is warm, but her breath is cold.



The Pastor’s Betrayal:





He pulls a dagger. "I’m sorry. But it’s the only way."



He slashes Lilith’s throat—black blood sprays. The Entity screams (a sound like a thousand voices moaning).



"Now it’s hungry."



The Entity’s Voice:

The walls bleed. The stained glass melts into eyes.



Sable’s Choice:





Run?



Fight?



Or… give in? (The Entity promises to restore Lilith if Sable joins it.)



Ending Hook: Crimson tackles Sable out of the way as the Pastor explodes—his body unraveling into sigils and shadow.

Sensory Focus:





Sound: The Entity’s voice is everywhere, like a hand inside her skull.



Touch: Lilith’s blood is thick, like oil.



⚔️ CHAPTER 7: THE FINAL RITUAL (POV: Crimson)

Setting: The cathedral is alive*. The pews writhe like snakes, the statues weep black tears.





The Pastor’s Last Stand:





He’s not dead. He’s part of the Entity now—his lower half is shadow.



"Kill me… or become me."



The Fight:





Crimson vs. Pastor: Brutal, fast. The Pastor fights like a dancer, his shadow-limb whipping like a scorpion’s tail.



Sable vs. Herself: She’s holding Lilith, who’s bleeding out. The Entity’s whispers are louder: "SAVE HER… BECOME HER…"



The Twist:





The only way to break the curse is for someone to love a bride back to humanity.



Sable kisses Lilith—not with lust, but with memory. "I never forgot you."



Lilith screams—her eyes clear. The Entity howls.



The Pastor’s Sacrifice:





Seeing this, he laughs. "Of course… it was always love."



He drives his dagger into his own heart. The Entity screams, the sigil shatters.



The Aftermath:





The town collapses. The catgirls scream, their armor cracking, their minds returning.



The moon goes dark. The bell rings once more—a clean, pure sound.

Sensory Focus:





Sight: The Entity’s form—a mass of eyes and mouths, recoiling like a burned thing.



Touch: The heat of Sable’s tears on Lilith’s face.



☀️ CHAPTER 8: DAWN (POV: Rotating)

Setting: The town at sunrise. The catgirls are human again—confused, naked, free.





The Reckoning:





The black-furred catgirl (Pastor’s lover) collapses, weeping. "I remember… I remember everything…"



Lilith wakes, disoriented. "Sable…?" (She doesn’t remember the Entity—just dreaming of her.)



Crimson’s Realization:





He sees Sable crying over Lilith. "You… loved her."



Sable snaps: "And I still do, you idiot!"

Sable: "Because I was scared you’d leave!"



The Resolution:





Crimson kisses her forehead. "I’m not that much of an asshole."



Lilith smiles weakly. "So… who’s he?"



Sable laughs through tears. "The biggest idiot I know."



The Future:





The town is free, but scarred. The catgirls will rebuild.



Sable and Crimson finally sleep together—not out of lust, but exhaustion and relief.



Last Line (Lilith, watching the sunrise): "…We should burn this place down."

Sensory Focus:





Smell: Fresh air, real for the first time in years.



Sound: Birds. Actual birds.
