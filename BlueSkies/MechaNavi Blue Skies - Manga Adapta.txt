**MechaNavi: Blue Skies - Manga Adaptation**  
**Page 1 - Cinematic Opening**  

---

**Panel 1 (Wide, Establishing Shot)**  
- Scene: A **massive fleet dock**, bathed in golden morning light, with towering ships in the background being prepped. The **MechaNavi** looms in the distance, partially silhouetted by the light.
- Atmosphere: **Calm before the storm**, the air is still, machinery hums softly in the background.
- Text Box (Navi's Thoughts):  
  *“Mornings ruin perfectly good dreams.”*

---

**Panel 2 (Medium Shot: Navi Relaxing)**  
- Scene: **Navi reclines lazily in a control chair**, boots propped up on the console, spinning a screwdriver between her fingers. A **holographic diagnostic screen** floats beside her, half-ignored.
- Expression: **Carefree, slightly mischievous**.
- Dialogue (Navi):  
  > “Crimson, tell me, why do we even have mornings?”

---

**Panel 3 (Medium Shot: Crimson, Arms Crossed)**  
- Scene: **Crimson stands near the bulkhead**, arms crossed, watching Navi with his usual **half-exasperated, half-amused** expression.
- Expression: **Mildly unimpressed but used to her antics.**
- Dialogue (Crimson):  
  > “Because some of us actually wake up to get things done.”  
  > *“Unlike you, who finds new and creative ways to avoid every bit of responsibility.”*

---

**Panel 4 (Close-Up: Navi’s Side Profile, Smirking)**  
- Scene: **Navi grinning, the morning light catching strands of her silver hair**.
- Expression: **Smug, playful.**
- Dialogue (Navi):  
  > “Oh please, responsibility is just another word for ‘boring’.”  
  > *“And this?”* (Gestures toward MechaNavi) *“This is freedom.”*

---

**Panel 5 (Close-Up: Crimson’s Eyes Narrowing, Thoughtful)**  
- Scene: **Crimson’s face, slightly shadowed as he watches Navi.**
- Expression: **A mix of concern and mild annoyance.**
- Dialogue (Crimson):  
  > “Freedom isn’t much use if you don’t live long enough to enjoy it.”

---

**Panel 6 (Tension Build-Up Panel)**  
- Scene: **A small warning light flickers on a nearby console.**
- Sound Effect: *BEEP.*
- Text Box: *Unscheduled approach detected.*

---

**Panel 7 (Close-Up: Navi, Smirk Fading Slightly)**  
- Scene: **Navi glancing at the screen, her expression shifting from amused to intrigued.**
- Expression: **Slightly surprised, but excited.**
- Dialogue (Navi):  
  > “Well… guess morning just got interesting.”

---

**Panel 8 (Final Panel: Alarm Blaring, Ship Rattling Slightly)**  
- Scene: **A red warning light flashes on the control panel.**
- Sound Effect: *WAAAH-WAAAH-WAAAH* (Loud klaxon blaring.)
- Atmosphere: **Tension spikes instantly. The ship is about to get rocked.**
