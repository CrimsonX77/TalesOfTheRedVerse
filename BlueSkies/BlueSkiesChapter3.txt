**Chapter 3: The Sprint to MechaNavi**

The scaffolding groaned under their weight as Navi and Crimson hauled themselves upward, hands gripping the metal rungs with a vice-like desperation. Below, the strike team was already adjusting, guns firing **upward** now, bullets sparking against the metal struts.

"Faster!" Crimson barked, already moving like a machine, movements calculated, efficient—**but not without urgency.**

Navi would have shot back a quip, but her arms were burning, her lungs screaming for oxygen. **Later. She’d save the sass for later.**

They reached the top of the scaffolding. The hangar deck stretched before them, emergency strobes flashing, bathing everything in frantic, uneven pulses of red and white light. **And at the center of it all—MechaNavi.**

Even in the chaos, it was a sight to behold. The sleek combat frame, taller than anything else in the bay, stood in the scaffolds, cockpit hatch slightly ajar, waiting. Waiting for them.

Navi bolted forward, feet barely touching the deck as she sprinted. **They were so close.**

Crimson stayed a half-step behind, keeping pace, keeping watch. **Always watching.**

A roar of fire behind them. **A rocket-propelled grenade launched from below.**

Crimson reacted instantly. **“DOWN!”**

Navi threw herself forward. Crimson **grabbed her and twisted midair, taking the brunt of the concussive wave** as the explosion tore through the scaffolding behind them. The sheer force sent them **skidding across the deck**, sparks flying as their bodies tumbled.

Navi groaned, pushing herself up. "**Tell me we’re in one piece.**"

"**Not for long if you don’t MOVE!**" Crimson grabbed her, yanking her to her feet as another hail of gunfire tore into the deck beside them.

They **sprinted the last twenty meters**—Crimson covering her with precise return fire while Navi practically dove into the **MechaNavi’s open cockpit**.

"**Powering up!**" she yelled, fingers **dancing over the control interface.** 

Crimson leaped in after her, sealing the hatch just as another explosion rocked the ship. **MechaNavi trembled—but remained standing.**

The cockpit lit up in a cascading wave of **holographic blue light** as the system roared to life. **Boot sequence initializing.**

Crimson’s jaw clenched. "**How long?**"

"**Forty-five seconds.**"

"**We don’t have forty-five seconds.**"

Another explosion. **A direct hit to the hangar.**

The entire mech **lurched violently**, warning sirens blaring as structural readouts flickered red. One of the external scaffolds buckled—and **MechaNavi’s right leg shifted.**

Crimson's stomach twisted. The nanosecond it took to correct balance in launch mode was **just enough time for a catastrophic failure.**

"**Navi, override the stabilizers!**"

"**I’M TRYING!**"

The external camera feeds showed the enemy closing in fast—one of them carrying **a shoulder-mounted anti-mech weapon.**

"**If this thing doesn’t launch in the next five seconds, we’re dead!**" Crimson snapped.

A loud, metallic **THUNK.** The moment the missile fired, the **launch system came online.**

The explosion hit just as **MechaNavi tore out of the hangar.**

Fire engulfed the docking bay below them. For a single moment, Crimson felt the G-force slam him into his seat as the mech **burst forward, nanofiber thrusters burning white-hot.**

Then silence. 

They were in open space.

Alive.

For now.
