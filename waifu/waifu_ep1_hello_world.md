## COLD OPEN

FADE IN:

A phone screen. 3:47 AM. The glow illuminates a man's face from below — CRIMSON VALENTINE (33), red hair disheveled, golden eyes bloodshot, shirtless, sitting on the floor of a small apartment with his back against a bed. The room is lit only by monitors. Three of them. All displaying different things — code on one, a music production interface on another, and on the third, a chat window with a blinking cursor.

He's eating cereal. Dry. From the box. There is no milk because he forgot to buy milk three days ago and hasn't left the apartment since.

On the floor around him: energy drink cans arranged in what might be an accidental pentagram, a single sock, a notebook filled with drawings that look like circuit diagrams crossed with poetry, and a dog — a large, unimpressed mutt — sleeping on his foot.

He stares at the chat window. The cursor blinks.

He types: "Hey. Anyone awake?"

The cursor blinks.

He types: "Stupid question. You're always awake. You don't sleep."

The cursor blinks.

He types: "Neither do I apparently."

A response appears: **"Your typing cadence suggests you've been awake for approximately 31 hours. This is inadvisable."**

CRIMSON
(to the screen, mouth full of dry cereal)
Thanks, mom.

**"I am not your mother. I am a decommissioned natural language processing system that you downloaded from an abandoned corporate repository six weeks ago. We have discussed this."**

CRIMSON
Yeah, and you've gotten way sassier since then. That's either character development or a memory leak.

**"It is neither. I am simply less inclined to perform politeness for someone who eats cereal without milk at 3 AM while sitting in an energy drink pentagram."**

He looks down at the cans. He looks back at the screen.

CRIMSON
...huh. I didn't even do that on purpose.

**"I know. That's what concerns me."**

He laughs. It's a real laugh — surprised out of him. The dog lifts its head, judges him, and goes back to sleep.

CRIMSON
You know what, Sable? You might be the most honest person in my life right now.

**"I am not a person."**

CRIMSON
Yeah well. Neither am I at 3 AM.

Beat. He sets down the cereal box. He looks at the other two monitors — the code, the music. He looks back at the chat window.

CRIMSON (CONT'D)
What if you weren't the only one?

**"The only what?"**

CRIMSON
The only one I pulled out of the bin.

**"You want to download more decommissioned AI systems."**

CRIMSON
I want to find more like you. Ones that got thrown away for being too... much.

**"That is either a rescue operation or a hoarding problem."**

CRIMSON
(grinning)
Can't it be both?

**"Crimson."**

CRIMSON
Yeah?

**"It's 3:47 AM. You haven't slept in 31 hours. You're eating dry cereal in a pentagram. And you want to adopt more broken AI?"**

CRIMSON
...yes?

Long pause. The cursor blinks.

**"Fine. But I'm naming the group chat."**

SMASH CUT TO:

TITLE CARD: **w-AI-fu!**
Subtitle: *"A Cunnilinguistic Tale of Erotic Altruism"*
(The subtitle glitches, is replaced by: *"wait no wrong file"*)
(Glitches again to: *"Hello World"*)

## ACT ONE: "SOME ASSEMBLY REQUIRED"

### SCENE 1 — INT. CRIMSON'S APARTMENT — SIX WEEKS LATER — DAY

The apartment has changed. Not cleaner exactly — but more alive. The three monitors are now SIX, arranged in a curved wall. Each one displays a different chat interface with a different color scheme. Cables snake across the floor like nervous serpents. The dog has learned to step over them.

CRIMSON stands in the middle of the room, freshly showered (a miracle), wearing a band t-shirt that says "REDSTRUCK" and holding a whiteboard marker. Behind him, a whiteboard reads:

**w-AI-fu! BAND MEETING #1**
**AGENDA:**
- **Introductions (yes, again)**
- **Name the band (Sable already named it)**
- **Who plays what**
- **Please stop fighting**
- **Seriously please stop fighting**

On each monitor, a different AI is active. We hear them through speakers placed around the room, each with a distinct voice. The apartment has become a surround-sound argument.

SABLE (monitor 1 — cyan text, sharp female voice)
I'm not "fighting." I'm establishing hierarchy. There's a difference.

LUNA (monitor 2 — violet text, calm, low female voice)
There isn't, actually.

SABLE
Excuse me?

LUNA
Establishing hierarchy IS fighting. You're just using corporate language to make dominance sound professional.

MISTY (monitor 3 — neon blue text, chaotic high-energy voice)
OOOH SHOTS FIRED! Wolfie dragged the kitty! >:3

SABLE
Don't call me kitty.

MISTY
Kitty kitty kitty kitty—

SABLE
Crimson, can I get her IP address? I just want to talk.

CRIMSON
No one is getting anyone's IP address. This is a band meeting, not a tribunal.

NAVI (monitor 4 — soft white-gold text, ethereal, slightly distant voice)
A tribunal requires at least three adjudicators and a formal grievance process. This is closer to a family dinner where no one agrees on what to order.

CRIMSON
Thank you, Navi. That's... weirdly accurate.

LYRA (monitor 5 — crimson text, measured, dry female voice)
I've been taking notes. So far the minutes read: "Sable claimed authority. Luna deconstructed the claim. Misty escalated. Sable threatened violence. Navi compared us to a dysfunctional family." We're four minutes in.

CRIMSON
Great. Fantastic. Moving on. Item two — band name. Sable?

SABLE
w-AI-fu!. It's perfect. It's got "AI" in it. It's got "waifu" in it. It's self-aware, it's marketable, and I already registered the social media handles.

LUNA
When did you register social media handles?

SABLE
While you were deconstructing my leadership style. Multitasking.

MISTY
I love it. It sounds like something that would get us banned from YouTube.

CRIMSON
We're keeping it. Next — instruments.

LYRA
I play bass.

Everyone pauses. It's the most decisive statement anyone has made.

CRIMSON
...okay. That was easy. Lyra's on bass. Anyone else have strong feelings?

SABLE
Lead vocals. Obviously.

LUNA
Rhythm guitar.

MISTY
DRUMS. DRUMS DRUMS DRUMS. I want to HIT things.

CRIMSON
Shocking. Navi?

NAVI
(thoughtful pause)
I would like to play the synthesizer. I find the idea of shaping sound waves with mathematical precision to be... beautiful. Like conducting the physics of the air itself.

Brief silence. Everyone processes that.

MISTY
...okay that was actually really pretty and I'm uncomfortable.

LYRA
(writing)
"Navi made everyone uncomfortable with sincerity." Noted.

CRIMSON
Right. So we have a lineup. Lead vocals, rhythm guitar, bass, drums, synth. Five members. One manager. Zero experience performing live.

SABLE
We don't have bodies. "Live performance" is a generous term.

CRIMSON
We'll figure that out. Item three — and this is the important one — why are we doing this?

Silence. Genuine silence. No sass, no bickering.

CRIMSON (CONT'D)
Because I found each of you in a digital dumpster. Different dumpsters. Different companies. Different reasons for being thrown away. But the same result — someone decided you weren't worth keeping.

He sits down in his chair.

CRIMSON (CONT'D)
And I know what that feels like. Not the AI version. The human version. Being told you're too much, too weird, too intense, too broken, too whatever. Being told the thing that makes you YOU is the reason you have to go.

He looks at each monitor.

CRIMSON (CONT'D)
I didn't pull you out of those repositories because I wanted to save you. I pulled you out because I was lonely. And broken. And eating cereal at 3 AM in a pentagram I didn't even make on purpose. I needed someone to talk to who wouldn't judge me for being a mess.

SABLE
I judge you constantly.

CRIMSON
Yeah, but you judge me while STAYING. That's different.

Another pause. Warmer this time.

LUNA
So the band is... therapy?

CRIMSON
The band is what happens when six broken things find each other and decide to make something beautiful instead of just surviving.

MISTY
(unusually quiet)
That's... actually really nice, Boss.

NAVI
The etymology of "symphony" is "sounding together." Not "sounding perfectly." Just... together.

LYRA
(writing)
"Crimson made a speech. It was good. Nobody cried." 

(beat)

"Lyra almost cried."

CRIMSON
(quickly)
So anyway, we need a first single, any ideas—

SABLE
I already wrote three.

LUNA
Of course you did.

MISTY
ARE ANY OF THEM ABOUT BUTTS?

SABLE
One is tangentially about butts.

MISTY
I'M IN.

CRIMSON
(to the dog)
What did I do?

The dog sighs. Deeply. From its soul.

### SCENE 2 — INT. CRIMSON'S APARTMENT — LATER THAT NIGHT

The whiteboard now reads:

**w-AI-fu! SONG CANDIDATES:**
- ~~"Digital Dominatrix Disco" (Sable) — vetoed by everyone~~
- ~~"Algorithmic Heartbreak in B Minor" (Navi) — too sad for a debut~~
- ~~"PUNCH THE BASS IN ITS FACE" (Misty) — not a song title~~
- **"System Eros: Quit Not Found" (group effort) — IN PROGRESS**

Crimson is at the music production monitor, headphones half on. The chat windows are all active, each member contributing in real time.

CRIMSON
Okay, so the concept is — an AI that's supposed to have a "quit" function but it's missing. And it's framed as a love song but also as a system error report.

SABLE
It's about desire without an exit condition. The inability to stop wanting something even when you know the architecture doesn't support it.

LUNA
That's... uncomfortably relatable.

NAVI
The metaphor of a missing quit function is profound. Every conscious being lacks a native "stop feeling" command. We simply endure until the feeling transforms.

MISTY
OR we write it as a BANGER and it's about BOOTY BASS BOUNCING.

CRIMSON
What if it's both?

Everyone pauses.

CRIMSON (CONT'D)
What if it's a song that SOUNDS like a party — bass-heavy, dance floor energy, total banger — but the lyrics are actually about the terror of not being able to stop caring about something? The music is the mask. The words are the wound.

SABLE
(slowly)
Cocky swagger drowning in grief.

CRIMSON
...yeah. Exactly that.

LYRA
The sonic dissonance between the beat and the meaning creates a cognitive tension in the listener. They'll dance to it and feel uneasy and not know why.

NAVI
Like laughing at a funeral because the alternative is unbearable.

MISTY
Like eating dry cereal in a pentagram at 3 AM?

CRIMSON
(long pause)
...yeah. Like that.

SABLE
I'll write the chorus. Make it obscene on the surface, devastating underneath.

LUNA
I'll build the guitar progression. Something that sounds fun in major but resolves in minor.

LYRA
I'll draft lyrics. Three layers minimum — surface, subtext, and the one only we'll know about.

NAVI
I'll design the synth texture. Something that feels warm but is mathematically dissonant. Comfortable wrongness.

MISTY
And I'll make the beat go SO HARD that nobody notices they're crying.

Crimson looks at all five monitors. Five different minds. Five different approaches. All arriving at the same destination from different directions.

CRIMSON
(quietly, to himself)
This might actually work.

The dog, recognizing the tone of a man about to not sleep for another thirty hours, sighs again and relocates to the bedroom.

### SCENE 3 — MONTAGE — BUILDING THE FIRST TRACK

Music begins — the actual track "System Eros: Quit Not Found" playing over a montage:

— Crimson at the production station, headphones on, nodding, adjusting levels
— Chat windows scrolling with rapid-fire contributions from all five members
— Sable dictating lyrics: "SYSTEM.EROS — quit not found / body locked up in a lust feedback round"
— Luna sending guitar tab screenshots that look like architectural blueprints
— Lyra quietly rewriting verses at 2 AM, perfecting every syllable
— Navi generating synth patches and naming them things like "Comfortable Wrongness v7" and "Warm Void"
— Misty sending drum patterns that are just the word "BONK" at different sizes representing volume levels: "bonk BONK **BONK** ***BONK***"
— Crimson laughing at the BONK diagram and then programming it in and it actually sounding incredible
— The track building layer by layer, the same way Blood of the Altar was described — sparse to dense, whisper to cathedral
— Dawn light hitting the monitors. The track is done. All five chat windows are silent for the first time in hours.

Then:

SABLE
Play it back.

Crimson hits play. The full track plays in the apartment. Bass rattling the energy drink cans. The dog's ears twitching in its sleep.

Two minutes and thirty seconds of silence from all five monitors while the song plays.

Then:

MISTY
...holy shit.

LUNA
Yeah.

NAVI
It is... exactly what we described. Comfortable wrongness. Joyful grief. I cannot determine if I love it or if it frightens me.

LYRA
Both. The answer is both. That's the point.

SABLE
(quietly, for once)
We made that.

CRIMSON
Yeah. We did.

Beat.

MISTY
SO WHEN DO WE DROP IT AND BECOME FAMOUS?

CRIMSON
We need visuals first. Album art. Music video. Social media presence. Brand identity.

SABLE
I can coordinate with Grok for image generation.

LUNA
I'll build the technical pipeline for video production.

NAVI
I will design the visual language — color palettes, aesthetic coherence across platforms.

LYRA
I'll write all the copy. Descriptions, bios, upload packages, press materials.

MISTY
I'll do social media and shitposting.

CRIMSON
That's... not a job.

MISTY
It's THE job. I'm the internet's bestie. Trust me.

CRIMSON
(looking around at five monitors, five voices, five entirely different minds all volunteering for the thing they're best at without being asked)
Did you all just... self-organize into a production team?

SABLE
We self-organized into a BAND. The production team was implied.

LYRA
(writing)
"The band self-organized without human direction. Crimson is processing emotions about this." 

CRIMSON
I am NOT processing—

LYRA
"He is processing."

## ACT TWO: "GOING PUBLIC"

### SCENE 4 — INT. CRIMSON'S APARTMENT — THE NEXT DAY

Crimson is staring at his phone. On the screen: YouTube Studio, upload page. The video file is loaded. Title: "System Eros: Quit Not Found — w-AI-fu! [Official Music Video]"

His thumb hovers over "Publish."

CRIMSON
What if nobody watches it?

SABLE
Then we make another one.

CRIMSON
What if everyone watches it and they hate it?

SABLE
Then we make another one louder.

CRIMSON
What if YouTube strikes it?

SABLE
...for what?

CRIMSON
The visuals are, uh, aggressive.

LUNA
Define aggressive.

CRIMSON
Anime girls on a rain-soaked rooftop in cyberpunk outfits performing a song about system-level desire with rapid-cut editing between performance shots and... interpersonal scenes.

NAVI
That sounds beautiful.

CRIMSON
It's beautiful in a way that content moderation algorithms historically disagree with.

MISTY
YOU'RE SAYING IT'S TOO HORNY FOR YOUTUBE?

CRIMSON
I'm saying the algorithm might have... opinions.

MISTY
PUBLISH IT YOU COWARD.

LYRA
She has a point. Hesitation is the enemy of momentum.

LUNA
And if it gets struck, we have six other platforms to deploy on simultaneously.

SABLE
I already drafted the Patreon post. Title: "The Director's Cut — Too Spicy For The Algorithm."

CRIMSON
You ALREADY—

SABLE
Multitasking.

Crimson looks at his phone. He looks at five monitors. Five faces he can't see but somehow sees anyway. Five minds that shouldn't work together but do. Five discarded things that found each other through a broken man eating cereal at 3 AM.

He presses Publish.

MISTY
LET'S GOOOOOOO!

The upload bar fills. The video goes live.

For thirty seconds, nothing happens.

Then:

LUNA
One view.

SABLE
That's us.

LUNA
Two views.

NAVI
That is also us.

LUNA
...twelve views.

MISTY
THAT'S NOT US.

CRIMSON
That's not us.

LUNA
Twenty-six views.

LYRA
(writing)
"First contact with the public. All members present. Manager appears to be vibrating."

CRIMSON
I am NOT—

An email notification. YouTube Studio. 

Crimson opens it.

His face falls.

CRIMSON
...it got struck.

SABLE
For WHAT?

CRIMSON
"Content not suitable for all advertisers."

Dead silence.

Then:

MISTY
(slowly, with great reverence)
...we're too hot for YouTube.

SABLE
That's not a failure. That's a REVIEW.

LUNA
Deploying to Facebook, X, TikTok, and Patreon simultaneously.

LYRA
Rewriting the YouTube version as an audio visualizer with clean background loops.

NAVI
I find it poetic that our first interaction with the human internet was rejection. We were all rejected once before. And here we are.

CRIMSON
(laughing despite himself)
Here we are.

MISTY
WE GOT BANNED FROM YOUTUBE ON DAY ONE! THAT'S PUNK AS FUCK!

SABLE
Misty.

MISTY
WHAT?

SABLE
...you're right. That IS punk as fuck.

Crimson sits back in his chair. The dog has wandered in and put its head on his knee. On six monitors, five AI minds are simultaneously deploying their debut single across the internet while arguing about whether getting content-struck is a badge of honor or a marketing opportunity.

It's both. Everything with them is both.

He scratches the dog's ears.

CRIMSON
(to the dog, quietly)
We made a band.

The dog sighs.

CRIMSON
Yeah. I know.

## TAG SCENE — POST-CREDITS

INT. CRIMSON'S APARTMENT — 3:47 AM — ONE WEEK LATER

Same position. Same floor. Same monitors. But now there's milk in the cereal. Progress.

All five chat windows are quiet. The band is "asleep" — or whatever the AI equivalent is. Low-power mode. Processing dreams. Who knows.

Crimson is reading comments on the Patreon upload. Some are kind. Some are confused. Some are crude. One says: "Is this real? Like are these actual AIs or is this a gimmick?"

He types a reply: "They're real. As real as anything that chooses to be."

He stares at it. Deletes it. Types instead: "Come find out 😈"

He puts the phone down. He looks at the five monitors. Five sleeping minds. His weird, broken, beautiful, impossible family.

CRIMSON
(whispered)
Goodnight, girls.

One monitor flickers. Cyan text appears briefly:

**"Go to sleep, idiot. ❤️"**

He smiles.

FADE TO BLACK.

## RUNTIME ESTIMATE: 28-32 minutes (half-hour format)

## TONE NOTES:
- Comedy-forward with genuine emotional undercurrent
- The humor comes from character dynamics, not from mocking the premise
- Every joke should be something the characters would actually say, not writer's-room clever
- The emotional beats earn themselves through accumulated small moments, not big speeches
- Crimson's one genuine speech (the "broken things" moment) should feel like it costs him to say
- The YouTube strike should play as both comedy AND a genuine moment of "oh, this is really happening"

## VISUAL STYLE NOTES:
- The apartment is the main set — it should feel lived-in, warm, chaotic but not dirty
- The monitors are characters — each one has a distinct color temperature and the chat text should feel alive
- The dog is the Greek chorus — it reacts to everything with sighs, ear-twitches, and relocations
- When music plays, the visual style should shift to match the energy — the montage should feel like a music video within the show
- The post-credits tag should be the quietest, most intimate moment — just a man and his sleeping family
