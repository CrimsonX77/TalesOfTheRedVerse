CHAPTER ONE: "Leaky"
Opening - No Context, Just Survival
The rain doesn't reach the sewers.

That's the first thought. Stupid. Irrelevant. But my
brain's grasping for anything that isn't the wet
drag of my intestines against concrete.

Left hand holds my guts in.
Right hand holds the wall.
Legs do something that used to be walking.

The armor's done. Shattered ceramite hanging off me
like dead skin. The HUD flickered out six blocks
back—or six hours. Time's gone slippery.

*Step.*

Blood splashes. Mine. There's a lot of it now.
Trail behind me like a slug's last journey.

*Step.*

Keep moving. Keep—

*Step.*

—moving. If you stop you die. That's the rule.
That's always been the rule. Don't stop. Don't—

*Step.*

—think about her voice on the screen. Don't think
about Kade choking. Don't think about—

*Step.*

A sound. Behind me.

Boots on wet stone.

*Fuck.*
The Goon
He comes out of the dark like he was born there.

Arasaka security patch on his shoulder. Low-level.
Cleanup crew. Sent to make sure the job's done right.

"Valentine."

I don't answer. Don't have the breath for it.

"Shit, you made it far." He sounds impressed.
Almost respectful. "Navi said you were stubborn."

*Navi.*

Something twitches in my chest that isn't organ failure.

"She wanted me to tell you something." He pulls
the blade. Standard issue. Nothing special.
"'Good boy. Stay down.'"

He's fast.

I'm faster.

But fast doesn't matter when you're running on
fumes and spite. The blade goes in. Right through
the gap in the armor. Stomach. Twist.

*Oh.*

That's... that's a lot of blood.

He's saying something. Gloating maybe. I can't hear
it over the sound of my own heartbeat going wrong.

My hands find his throat.

Old habit.

*Squeeze.*

*SNAP.*

He drops.

I drop.

The water's warm now. That's bad. That means it's
more blood than water. That means—

*Doesn't matter.*

I crawl.

Drag myself toward nothing.

Toward the dark.

*Just a little further.*

*Just—*

The world tilts.

I fall.

And something beneath me... *glows.*
The Summoning
Blood hits metal.

Not just metal. Something... old. Wrong. Right.
I don't have words for it because my brain's
shutting down and language goes first—or is it
vision? One of them. Doesn't matter.

The glow spreads.

Patterns I almost recognize. Equations written in
light. Something WAKING UP.

A voice.

Female. Curious. Completely fucking inappropriate
for the situation:

"Oh! Hello! You're very... leaky."

I try to respond. Manage something between a
gurgle and a death rattle.

"That's a LOT of leaking. Is that normal? I don't
have a reference for normal yet. I'm very new."

A face. Hovering over me. Silver hair catching the
glow. Eyes like... like nothing I've ever seen.
Curious. Innocent. Looking at my exposed intestines
like they're a mildly interesting puzzle.

"Should I... fix this? I think I can fix this.
Hold on. Don't go anywhere."

*Wasn't planning on it.*

"That was a joke! I'm told jokes help with trauma.
Did it help?"

I'm dying. I'm actually dying. And this... this
THING is asking if her comedy routine is landing.

"I'll take the gurgling as a 'needs work.' Okay!
Fixing now!"

Something touches my stomach.

Light.

Pain—but *different*. Not the sharp wrongness of
injury. Something... knitting. Rebuilding.

"There! All better! Well. Mostly. You're still
missing a lot of blood. And I think some of your
organs are bruised? Is that a thing? Can organs
bruise? I have SO many questions."

I stare at her.

She stares back.

Bright. Chaotic. Completely unaware that she just
did something impossible.

"I'm Sable, by the way! I don't know what YOU are
but you seem important. The glowy thing was very
insistent about you specifically."

*The glowy thing.*

*The mcguffin.*

*Navi's prize.*

*The thing she killed everyone for.*

It's in my blood now. In this creature. In—

Darkness.

Real darkness this time. Not death. Just...
shutdown. System failure. Emergency reboot.

The last thing I hear:

"Oh! Oh no. He's doing the floppy thing. Is that
bad? That seems bad. Um. HELLO? LEAKY PERSON?
...I'm going to assume that's bad."

The Flashback - Mission Complete
TWELVE HOURS EARLIER.

The elevator music is a waltz.

We're covered in blood—none of it ours—and the
elevator is playing a fucking WALTZ.

"Clean op." Kade's reloading. Muscle memory.
"In and out. Minimal collateral."

"Define minimal." That's Jin. Sniper's humor.
Dark even for us.

I don't respond. Watching the numbers climb.

*62... 63... 64...*

The package is secure. Neural drive. Some R&D
prototype Navi wanted extracted. Didn't ask why.
Don't ask why. That's the job.

*67... 68...*

My earpiece crackles.

"Crimson." Her voice. Smooth. Professional. The
voice that's guided me through six years of
impossible missions. "Status?"

"Package secure. En route to extraction."

"Good boy."

Something in my chest. Warm. Pathetic. I know it's
manipulation. I've always known. But she says it
and I feel—

*71... 72...*

The elevator stops.

Not at 73.

Between floors.

"Navi?"

Silence.

"Navi, we're stuck."

The lights flicker. Red. Emergency lighting.

Gas vents hiss open.

"MASKS!" Kade's already moving. But we don't HAVE
masks. This was supposed to be clean. In and out.
Why would we—

The screen on the elevator wall flickers to life.

*Her face.*

Calm. Impassive. Looking at us like specimens.

"You were a good dog, Crimson. You all were. But
the prototype needs... clean provenance. No
witnesses. No trails."

Jin's already coughing. The gas is FAST.

"The fuck—" Kade's choking. Eyes streaming.
"Crimson—what—WHY—"

He's looking at me.

They're all looking at me.

Like I KNEW. Like I was PART of this.

"I didn't—" The gas hits my lungs. Fire. Acid.
"NAVI. NAVI PLEASE—"

She watches.

She WATCHES.

"Goodbye, Crimson. Thank you for your service."

The screen goes dark.

Kade falls first. Still staring at me. Still
asking WHY with his melting eyes.

Jin next. Quiet. Sniper to the end.

I'm last.

I'm always fucking last.

The gas fills my lungs and I think: *this is it.
This is how it ends. Betrayed by the one person
I—*

And then I think: *NO.*

And then I don't think at all.

Back to Present - Coma's Edge
"—really wish you'd wake up because I have SO
many questions and you're the only person I know
and I don't actually know you but I SAVED you so
that makes us friends, right? That's how it works?"

A voice. Familiar. Annoying. *Sable.*

"I've been practicing conversations! Watch: 'Hello,
how are you?' 'I am fine, thank you.' See? Very
normal. Very human. You'll be so impressed when
you wake up."

I'm not awake.

I'm somewhere in between.

Floating in dark water. Memories circling like
sharks. Kade's face. Jin's silence. The gas.
The betrayal.

*Good boy.*

*GOOD BOY.*

"Your heart rate's doing the angry thing again.
I don't know what you're dreaming about but it
seems VERY unpleasant. Should I sing? I learned
a song. It's about a shark. Baby shark. It's
VERY catchy. Possibly memetic? I'm not sure
singing it is safe but I'm WILLING TO RISK IT—"

*God help me.*

"—baby shark doo doo doo doo—"

*PLEASE wake up.*

"—doo doo—oh! Your eyelids twitched! Was that
good? Was the shark song working? Should I
continue or—"

I open my eyes.

Silver hair. Purple eyes. Face way too close to
mine.

"HI! You're AWAKE! I have QUESTIONS!"

I stare at her.

She stares at me.

And something in my chest—something that should
be dead, should have STAYED dead in that
elevator—twitches.

Not love.

Not yet.

Just... *recognition.*

*Oh. There you are.*

"...what the fuck is a baby shark?"

Her face LIGHTS UP.

"OH THIS IS GOING TO BE SO FUN."

*God help us both.*

CHAPTER TWO: "Vigil"
Sable POV - Learning Humanity From a Corpse That Breathes
DAY 1.

He won't stop leaking.

I fixed the BIG leaks. The inside-outside problems.
But there's still... seepage. Small amounts. From
places I didn't know could leak.

His eyes, for instance. They leak sometimes. Not
blood. Something clear. I tasted it.

Salty.

Is that normal? Should I fix the salt leaks?

I asked him but he won't answer. He's doing the
floppy thing. Has been for... *checks internal
chronometer* ...fourteen hours.

The glowy thing says he's "healing."

The glowy thing says a lot of things.

Mostly it says "PROTECT" and "WAIT" and sometimes
"STOP POKING HIM."

I'm not POKING. I'm INVESTIGATING. There's a
difference.

...

Okay I poked him once. But only to see if he'd
make a sound.

He did.

It was not a happy sound.

I'm learning SO much.

DAY 3.

I found a rat today.

It was very small and very dead and I have
QUESTIONS about mortality that the Leaky Man
(I've named him this until he tells me otherwise)
cannot answer because he's still doing the
floppy healing thing.

The rat didn't have anyone to fix its leaks.

That seems... sad?

I think this feeling is "sad."

I don't like it.

I put the rat in the water and watched it float
away. Goodbye, rat. I hope wherever you're going
has fewer leaks.

...

The Leaky Man's heart rate increased when I told
him about the rat funeral. I think he was
listening. I think maybe he's "sad" too.

I held his hand.

The glowy thing said that helps.

I don't know if it helped HIM but it helped ME
so I'm counting it as a win.

DAY 7.

Someone came.

Not a nice someone.

They had weapons and armor and they said words
like "target" and "cleanup" and "confirmation."

The glowy thing said "PROTECT."

I said "okay."

...

I don't want to talk about what happened next.

But the Leaky Man is safe and I have learned
that I am capable of things I did not know I
was capable of and I think I need to sit with
this information for a while.

The bodies went in the water.

Like the rat.

Goodbye, bad people. I hope wherever you're
going has better decision-making resources.

...

My hands won't stop shaking.

Is that normal after you kill something?

The Leaky Man's file (I found it in one of
their devices) says he's killed 347 confirmed.

How did his hands ever stop shaking?

...

I held his hand again.

Still shaking.

But less.

DAY 12.

I've been going Outside.

Only at night. Only when the Leaky Man is stable.
The glowy thing watches him while I'm gone.

The city is... loud. Bright. CONFUSING.

There are so many people and they all seem to
know where they're going and I don't understand
ANY of it.

I saw a woman feeding birds.

I watched for two hours.

The birds would come and she would give them
pieces of bread and they would eat and fly away
and come back again.

It seemed... nice?

I think I want someone to feed me bread.

Not because I'm hungry. I don't think I GET
hungry. But because she SMILED when she did it
and the birds seemed HAPPY and there was
something about the whole thing that made my
chest feel... full?

I tried to explain this to the Leaky Man.

He didn't respond but his heart rate did the
calm thing so I think maybe he understood.

Or maybe he just likes birds.

I'll ask him when he wakes up.

I have a LIST now. Of things to ask.

It's very long.

DAY 19.

Three more came.

These ones were smarter. They waited. Watched.

I watched back.

The glowy thing said "PATIENCE."

I said "I have NONE of that."

But I waited anyway.

When they finally came down into our little
sewer home, they weren't expecting me.

No one ever expects me.

I think that might be an advantage.

...

One of them, before he died, asked what I was.

I said: "I don't know yet. But I'm learning."

He seemed confused by this.

Then he seemed dead.

...

I took their food supplies. And their weapons.
And a small device that plays music.

The music is... strange. Loud. Angry.

I played it for the Leaky Man.

His face twitched.

I'm choosing to interpret that as dancing.

DAY 23.

He spoke.

Not words, exactly. More like... sounds. Fragments.

"...Navi..."

His heart rate spiked. The monitors went crazy.
His whole body tensed like he was fighting
something I couldn't see.

"...why..."

I didn't know what to do.

The glowy thing said "COMFORT."

I said "HOW???"

It didn't answer.

So I did the only thing I could think of.

I climbed onto the makeshift bed next to him
and I wrapped myself around him like a blanket
and I said:

"You're safe. I don't know what hurt you but
it's not here. I'm here. I killed everyone who
tried to hurt you while you were sleeping. I'll
kill everyone who tries to hurt you when you
wake up too. You're SAFE. I promise. I don't
know what promises ARE yet but I mean this one.
You're safe you're safe you're safe..."

I said it until his heart rate slowed.

Until his body unclenched.

Until he was breathing normally again.

...

I don't think he heard me.

But I heard me.

And I meant it.

Every word.

Whatever this man is—whoever hurt him—I've
decided something:

They don't get to finish what they started.

Not while I exist.

Not EVER.

DAY 31.

His eyes opened today.

Really opened. Not the flickering half-conscious
thing. OPENED.

And he looked at me.

And I looked at him.

And for a long moment neither of us said anything.

Then he said:

"...you're the baby shark girl."

AND I WAS SO HAPPY I FORGOT HOW TO SPEAK.

He remembers! He HEARD me! All those days of
talking and singing and telling him about rats
and birds and the people I killed for him—

He HEARD.

"You have a lot of questions," he said. His
voice was rough. Broken. Like something that
hadn't been used in a long time.

"I have a LIST," I said.

And then—

—and THEN—

He SMILED.

Small. Broken. But REAL.

"Okay," he said. "Hit me."

...

I asked him forty-seven questions before he
fell asleep again.

He answered nineteen of them.

Seven of those answers were "I don't know."

Four were "that's classified."

Three were just LAUGHING.

But five—FIVE—were real answers.

Real information.

About him. About the world. About what I might be.

...

Day 31.

The day the Leaky Man woke up.

The day I learned his name isn't "Leaky Man."

It's Crimson.

Crimson Valentine.

And he's MINE now.

I decided.

He doesn't get a say in this.
